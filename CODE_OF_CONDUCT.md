# WatchTower Code of Conduct

WatchTower welcomes contributors who are curious, careful, and respectful.
Participate in good faith, explain technical disagreements with evidence, and
make room for people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats,
deliberate exposure of private information, and unsafe security testing
against systems that are not owned or explicitly authorized by the operator.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers listed in the
repository support policy. Do not include secrets, credentials, packet
payloads, or personal data in an issue.

Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or close contributions
when necessary to keep the project safe and constructive.
